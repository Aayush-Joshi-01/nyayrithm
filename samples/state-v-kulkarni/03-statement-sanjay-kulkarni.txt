STATEMENT OF THE ACCUSED
Recorded in the presence of his counsel, 6 March 2026

Name: Sanjay Kulkarni, aged 41, Chief Accountant

1. I did not steal anything and I had no dishonest intention. The Rs 18,00,000 was a short loan
   to my brother Vikram for a deposit on his shop. Mr Mehra agreed to it.

2. On the evening of 1 March, at about 9 pm, I called Mr Mehra's mobile and he answered. He said
   he was in a meeting and could not talk long, and told me: "Do what is needed, we will see on
   Monday." I understood that to mean I could go ahead with the loan, which we had discussed in
   the office in February.

3. At night, around 11:50 pm, I prepared the transfer from my home on the company's net-banking.
   The second approval came through on the same night. I assumed Mr Mehra had approved it with
   his token. I did not use his token and I do not have it.

4. I sent Mr Mehra a WhatsApp message on the morning of 2 March telling him the money had been
   sent to Vikram and would be returned by 10 March.

5. My brother returned Rs 6,00,000 to the company account on 9 March. He signed a written
   acknowledgement of the loan. The balance was to follow after his shop deposit was released.

6. Supplier Shree Ganesh Dyers has since been paid from the company's overdraft, I understand.
   I am ready to make good any shortfall.
