STATEMENT OF RECORD
Recorded by the investigating officer (ASI R. Chauhan) under section 180 of the BNSS, 2023
Date: 5 March 2026

Witness: Rohit Mehra, aged 52, Director, Mehra Textiles Pvt. Ltd.

1. I run the company with my wife, who is the other director. Sanjay Kulkarni joined as a clerk
   in 2017 and became chief accountant in 2021.

2. Our bank account requires two authorisers for any transfer above Rs 5,00,000. Sanjay prepares
   the payment (the maker). The second approval (the checker) must come from me or from my wife
   using our security tokens. I keep my token in my briefcase. My wife's token is at home.

3. I was in Surat from the afternoon of 28 February until the evening of 2 March for my nephew's
   wedding and a visit to our mill. My phone was mostly off because of the ceremonies and poor
   network. I did not speak to Sanjay on the phone on 1 March. I did not message him.

4. I did not approve a transfer of Rs 18,00,000 to Vikram Kulkarni. I cannot explain how the
   second approval was recorded. My token was with me in Surat.

5. Sanjay has never asked me for a loan before. Two years ago he asked for a salary advance of
   Rs 50,000 and I gave it, in writing.

6. On 2 March, after I returned in the evening, I opened WhatsApp and saw messages from Sanjay.
   I was upset and called him immediately. He said we would "sort it out on Monday".
