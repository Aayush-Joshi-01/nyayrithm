Offence alleged : Criminal breach of trust by a person entrusted with property (BNS, section 316)

Complainant : Rohit Mehra, Director, Mehra Textiles Pvt. Ltd.

Details of the information
I am the director of Mehra Textiles Pvt. Ltd. Our accountant, Sanjay Kulkarni, has worked for us
for nine years and was responsible for supplier payments. On 28 February 2026 I handed him the
payment schedule for our dye suppliers, including Rs 18,00,000 due to Shree Ganesh Dyers.

On the morning of 3 March our supplier called to say the payment had not arrived. I checked the
company's net-banking record and found that Rs 18,00,000 had been transferred on the night of
1 March 2026 to an account in the name of Vikram Kulkarni, who I now know is the accountant's
younger brother. I never authorised any transfer to Vikram Kulkarni. I did not give Sanjay any
loan, and I never told him he could use company money for anything but supplier payments.

When I confronted Sanjay on 3 March he said I had agreed to a short loan "on the phone". That is
untrue. I was travelling from Surat and my phone was switched off for much of 1 and 2 March.

I request that action be taken against Sanjay Kulkarni.
