Counsel for the State relies on Section 316 of the BNS and, in the alternative, Section 318 BNS.
The accused is charged under Section 406 IPC and Section 9999 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita.
On electronic evidence see Section 63 of the BSA, and Arjun Panditrao Khotkar v. Kailash
Kushanrao Gorantyal (2020) 7 SCC 1. The defence cites Ramesh Kumar v. State of Haryana
(1978) 1 SCC 248 and Rajesh Verma v. State of Nowhere (2019) 4 SCC 123 for the proposition that
a verbal loan is not breach of trust. Article 21 of the Constitution and Article 500 are also
invoked.
