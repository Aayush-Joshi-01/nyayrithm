# Vision and aims

## The aim

Make rigorous case preparation something a firm can do on any matter, on any day, instead of
once, expensively, before trial.

Nyayrithm gives a lawyer an opposing side that has read the whole file, argues in role,
cannot be flattered, and shows its sources. The lawyer's own judgment stays in charge; the
platform's job is to make that judgment better informed.

## Principles

**1. The record is the ground truth.** Agents argue from the evidence in the case, not from
what a model happens to remember. A claim with no source is marked as inference. A
claim that contradicts the record is surfaced, not smoothed over.

**2. Roles are boundaries, not costumes.** A witness knows only what the witness saw. A judge
sees what is on the record. Retrieval enforces this; the prompt does not merely ask nicely.

**3. Distrust the model's law.** Language models are fluent about statutes they have partly
invented. So citations are checked by fixed rules against a reference pack, not by another
model, and a wrong one is called out to the agent that made it.

**4. Everything is accountable.** Which model said what, from which prompt, retrieving which
passages, is hashed and chained. A human edit is recorded as an edit, with both versions
fingerprinted, and never silently replaces what the agent said.

**5. A firm's work is its own.** Isolation between firms is enforced in the data layer, and
the people who run the platform have no route to a firm's case content.

**6. Say what it cannot do.** Every result carries the limits that apply to it.

## What it is for

- **Preparing a matter:** run the hearing several ways, find the weak exhibit and the
  witness who will not hold up.
- **Cross-examination and advocacy practice:** take a seat yourself, or edit what an agent
  said and replay from there.
- **Training:** moot courts, clinics and chambers can give juniors realistic, repeatable
  hearings.
- **Strategy:** a private session with no judge, where counsel, an investigator and an expert
  weigh options.

## What it is not

- **Not legal advice**, and not a substitute for a lawyer's judgment.
- **Not a predictor of verdicts.** Simulated outcomes depend on the models and the prompts.
  Treat them as stress tests, not forecasts.
- **Not a legal database.** The jurisdiction packs index provisions and landmark cases so
  citations can be checked; they are not a replacement for the official texts.
- **Not an e-filing or case-management system**, today.
- **Not a place to put anything you could not put in front of your chosen model provider.**
  Evidence text is sent to the LLM you configure. Local models (Ollama) keep it on your
  hardware.
